# Mercado Libre Yellow

> Latin America's marketplace energy — Turbo yellow brand moments and bright-blue CTAs riding a deep Astronaut navy ground, packed into dense, trust-building product grids.

## Origin

MercadoLibre was founded in 1999 in Buenos Aires by Marcos Galperín and Hernán Kazah, and grew into the dominant e-commerce and fintech super-app of Latin America. What began as a regional auction-and-marketplace site expanded across the 2010s into Mercado Pago (payments), Mercado Envíos (logistics), and a mobile-first app used by hundreds of millions — the closest thing the region has to a combined Amazon and PayPal. The brand's most recognizable asset is its yellow handshake logomark, a symbol of agreement and trust between buyer and seller.

The visual identity pairs a bright "Turbo" yellow (PMS 803C) with a deep "Astronaut" navy (PMS 3591C), the field the handshake logo sits on. Across web and app chrome the system reads as dense product grids, navy headers, clean white product cards, and bright-blue call-to-action buttons. It is unapologetically information-rich retail UI: high-contrast, geometric, friendly, and engineered to surface as many listings, prices, ratings, and shipping promises as a screen can hold.

## Overview

Composition cues:
- **Layout**: Dense marketplace grid — product-card tiles packed into responsive columns, navy chrome bars at top, yellow brand banners and ribbons threading through. Information-rich, not sparse.
- **Content width**: Wide — marketplace pages stretch toward the viewport edges to maximize listing density; container caps only on editorial detail pages.
- **Framing**: Solid — flat saturated color blocks, navy chrome, and clean white cards with light shadows. No glass, no heavy ornament.
- **Grid intensity**: Strong — a tight 4px rhythm governs compact card tiles, price chips, star ratings, and shipping badges.

## Colors

The palette is built on a single decisive pairing: deep "Astronaut" navy (`#2D3277`) as the page ground, and bright "Turbo" yellow (`#FFE600`) as the signature brand accent. The navy is the field behind the yellow handshake logo and across all app chrome — it must stay deep and saturated, never muddy. Yellow is too light to be a canvas (it reads cream), so it appears only as brand moments: banners, ribbons, logo lockups, and highlight chips. Bright CTA blue (`#3483FA`) carries every action button and link, while clean white (`#FFFFFF`) holds the product cards and content surfaces that float on the navy. A deeper navy (`#1F2C73`) supplies panel depth and dividers.

**Role usage**:
- Page background → `colors.background.page` (Astronaut navy `#2D3277`, the dominant ground)
- Content surface / product cards → `colors.background.surface` (white `#FFFFFF`)
- Panels / chrome depth → `colors.background.subtle` (deeper navy `#1F2C73`)
- Brand moments / banners / highlights → `colors.primary.500` (Turbo yellow `#FFE600`)
- Primary actions / CTA buttons / links → `colors.accent.500` (CTA blue `#3483FA`)
- Navy field / secondary chrome → `colors.secondary.500` (Astronaut navy `#2D3277`)
- Body text on navy → `colors.text.primary` (white `#FFFFFF`)
- Body text on white cards → `colors.text.inverse` (navy `#2D3277`)

## Typography

The type voice is clean, geometric retail sans — friendly, legible at small sizes, and dense enough to carry listing copy. **Montserrat** drives headings as the closest free geometric pairing to the period brand sans **Proxima Nova**, lending wide, confident, slightly rounded display caps. **Open Sans** handles the high-volume marketplace body copy — product titles, prices, seller names, shipping lines — where neutral legibility at small sizes matters most. **Roboto Mono** covers the rare monospace need (order IDs, codes). No serifs anywhere: this is a modern, information-dense geometric system.

**Text styles**:
- `display-xl` — Montserrat, 96px, weight 800, line-height 1.0, letter-spacing -0.04em
- `display-lg` — Montserrat, 64px, weight 700, line-height 1.1, letter-spacing -0.02em
- `heading-1` — Montserrat, 36px, weight 700, line-height 1.2, letter-spacing -0.02em
- `body-lg` — Open Sans, 18px, weight 400, line-height 1.5, letter-spacing 0
- `body-md` — Open Sans, 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.5, letter-spacing 0
- `caption` — Open Sans, 13px, weight 600, line-height 1.375, letter-spacing 0
- `mono-md` — Roboto Mono, 14px, weight 400, line-height 1.5, letter-spacing 0

## Spacing & Layout

- Base unit: 4px; primary rhythm on a tight 4px increment for compact card tiles
- Scale: 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 / 96 / 128 px
- Container max-widths: sm 640px, md 768px, lg 1024px, xl 1280px — wide content for grid density
- Section padding: 64px vertical (md), 96px (lg) on editorial pages; tighter on listing grids
- Grid gaps: 16px (md) between product cards — close enough to read as a dense marketplace, open enough to separate tiles

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is flat and functional, not skeuomorphic. White product cards lift off the navy ground with light, cool-toned shadows; the navy chrome stays flush. Yellow brand moments are flat saturated color, never glossy. The whole system reads as clean retail UI — shadows exist to separate cards from the busy ground, not to dramatize.

- Surface style: solid (flat saturated color, no glass)
- Blur: none
- Shadow ladder: cool navy-tinted rgba — `rgba(16, 18, 35, ...)` at ascending opacity, lifting white cards off the navy field
- Inner shadow: subtle navy inset for recessed input fields
- Focus ring: 3px CTA-blue glow at 40% opacity (`rgba(52, 131, 250, 0.40)`)

## Shapes

- `none`: 0 — flush chrome bars and banners
- `sm`: 4px — chips, badges, star-rating pills
- `md`: 6px — buttons, inputs (the marketplace's friendly rounded-but-tight corner)
- `lg`: 8px — product cards and content panels
- `xl`: 12px — feature cards, promo modules
- `full`: 9999px — avatars, circular icon buttons, status dots

## Motion

Motion is lively but functional — quick, snappy feedback that keeps a busy marketplace feeling responsive without showing off. Cards lift on hover, CTAs tint and lift, links underline. Transitions are short and direct, matching the fast browse-and-buy rhythm of mobile commerce.

- Level: lively
- Durations: instant 0ms, fast 120ms, normal 250ms, slow 400ms, slower 600ms
- Default easing: `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` — smooth and responsive
- Spring easing: `cubic-bezier(0.34, 1.56, 0.64, 1)` for playful card lifts
- Hover patterns: lift (card raise), tint (CTA color shift), scale (subtle thumbnail zoom), underline (links)
- `reducedMotion: true` — all animations respect `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Do's & Don'ts

### ✓ Do
- Use Astronaut navy `#2D3277` as the dominant page ground — it is the field the yellow logo sits on
- Reserve Turbo yellow `#FFE600` for brand moments (banners, ribbons, logo, highlight chips), never the canvas
- Use CTA blue `#3483FA` for action buttons and links — blue carries every primary action
- Float clean white product cards on the navy with light cool shadows for dense, trust-building grids
- Pair Montserrat headings with Open Sans body for a clean geometric retail voice

### ✗ Don't
- Use cream, ivory, or plain-white page backgrounds — yellow reads cream, so the ground is navy `#2D3277`
- Wash the page in pale yellow — yellow is an accent, not the canvas
- Use serif body type — this is a clean retail geometric sans (Montserrat / Open Sans)
- Adopt a luxury, minimal, or sparse layout — the look is dense and information-rich
- Let the navy go desaturated or muddy, or the yellow go dull — keep navy deep and yellow vivid

## Applications

This system is ideal for e-commerce marketplaces, retail product catalogues, fintech and payments dashboards, and any dense, listing-heavy commerce UI serving Latin American or mass-market audiences. It excels where trust, density, and fast browse-and-buy flows matter — product grids, seller storefronts, checkout and payment screens, and promo landing pages — pairing navy chrome and white cards with yellow brand moments and bright-blue CTAs.
