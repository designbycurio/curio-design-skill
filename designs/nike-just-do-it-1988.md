# Nike "Just Do It" (1988)

> Black ground, white Swoosh, three words in Futura Condensed — move or get out of the way.

## Origin

Nike began in 1964 as Blue Ribbon Sports, co-founded by Phil Knight and coach Bill Bowerman in Oregon. In 1971, Portland State student Carolyn Davidson was paid $35 to sketch the Swoosh — a single motion mark she described as simply "a gesture of speed." The brand became Nike, and through the 1970s and early 1980s it sharpened its identity around athlete endorsements, Tinker Hatfield's Air Max and Air Jordan designs, and a house typography built on Futura Bold Condensed.

In 1988, Portland agency Wieden+Kennedy delivered the three words that would define the next four decades: "Just Do It." Coined by Dan Wieden (reportedly inspired by the last words of executed killer Gary Gilmore), the line paired with black-and-white athlete photography created a voice of raw imperative. The visual system hardened around it: full-bleed hero crops, ALL-CAPS condensed display type, the Swoosh as sole ornament, and a palette that is almost entirely monochrome — color only enters as a punch.

## Overview

Composition cues:
- **Layout**: full-bleed hero photography with a strict secondary grid for product and copy
- **Content width**: `full-bleed` at hero; `container` (max ~1280px) for copy and product rows
- **Framing**: `minimal` — no cards, no outlines, no glass; image and type float on black
- **Grid intensity**: `soft` — 12-column underneath, but rarely visible; asymmetric hero takes 60–70%
- **Rhythm**: 8px base, generous whitespace between sections (96–128px)

## Colors

The palette is deliberately monochrome-dominant: a near-black page (`#111111`) carries every surface, pure white is the primary ink and the Swoosh, and color appears only as a single deliberate punch — `#CDDC39` Volt on performance moments, `#C41E3A` sport red on heritage moments. Photography supplies all additional chroma. The rule is that black is the stage, white is the voice, and everything else is an accent used at most once per composition.

**Role usage**:
- Page background → `colors.background.page` (`#111111`)
- Surface / elevated cards → `colors.background.surface` (`#1A1A1A`)
- Subtle divider fills → `colors.background.subtle` (`#222222`)
- Primary text, Swoosh, CTA fill → `colors.primary.500` (`#FFFFFF`)
- Secondary body text → `colors.text.secondary` (`#999999`)
- Performance accent (badges, links, focus) → `colors.accent.500` Volt (`#CDDC39`)
- Heritage / danger accent → `colors.secondary.500` Sport Red (`#C41E3A`)
- Hairline borders → `colors.borders.default` (`#333333`)

## Typography

Type is the second loudest element after the photograph. Headlines are always condensed, always uppercase, always tight-tracked — Bebas Neue at hero scale, Barlow Condensed 800/900 as the workhorse subhead. Body drops into DM Sans or Inter for readability. Italics are avoided; emphasis comes from size and weight, not style.

**Text styles**:
- `display-xl` — Bebas Neue, 96px, weight 400 (Bebas has one weight), line-height 0.95, letter-spacing -0.02em, uppercase
- `display-lg` — Bebas Neue, 72px, weight 400, line-height 0.95, letter-spacing -0.02em, uppercase
- `heading-1` — Barlow Condensed, 48px, weight 900, line-height 1.0, letter-spacing -0.02em, uppercase
- `heading-2` — Barlow Condensed, 32px, weight 800, line-height 1.05, letter-spacing -0.02em, uppercase
- `heading-3` — Barlow Condensed, 24px, weight 700, line-height 1.1, letter-spacing -0.01em, uppercase
- `eyebrow` — Barlow Condensed, 12px, weight 700, line-height 1.2, letter-spacing 0.15em, uppercase
- `body-lg` — DM Sans, 18px, weight 400, line-height 1.6, letter-spacing 0
- `body-md` — DM Sans, 16px, weight 400, line-height 1.6, letter-spacing 0
- `caption` — DM Sans, 12px, weight 500, line-height 1.4, letter-spacing 0.02em
- `mono-md` — JetBrains Mono, 14px, weight 400, line-height 1.5, letter-spacing 0

## Spacing & Layout

- Base unit: **4px**; component rhythm **8px**
- Scale: 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128 px
- Container: `xl` 1280px for copy rows; hero sections run **edge-to-edge (full-bleed)**
- Section padding: 96–128px top/bottom on desktop, 32–64px on mobile
- Grid: 12 columns, 24–48px gutters, asymmetric hero slots (7-col image / 5-col copy)

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is material: flat matte surfaces, no shadows, no gradients, no glass. Hierarchy is built through scale (massive type vs. small caption), crop (hero vs. edge-aligned copy), and negative space. When elements "float," they float on black — not on shadow.

- Surface style: **flat** — matte black and near-black only
- Blur: **none** — nothing frosted, nothing soft
- Shadow ladder: `none` at every step; the only "shadow" is a Volt focus ring for accessibility

## Shapes

- Every border-radius is **0** — buttons, inputs, cards, images are all sharp-edged
- The sole curved form in the system is the **Swoosh** itself, and a limited set of swoosh-derived dividers
- Lines are always 1–2px, never hairline softer than 1px; separators are solid `#333333`

## Motion

Motion temperament is **decisive and camera-like** — think cut, zoom, reveal rather than bounce or wiggle. Transitions are fast (120–250ms) with sharp easing. Hero images use subtle Ken Burns scale; CTAs punch into Volt on hover; links reveal a hard underline. Nothing oscillates; nothing "plays."

- Level: **lively** (imperative, not playful)
- Durations: instant 0ms, fast 120ms, normal 250ms, slow 400ms, slower 600ms
- Easings: default `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)`; out `cubic-bezier(0,0.55,0.45,1)`; spring reserved for hero reveals only
- Hover patterns: **underline** (links), **scale** 1.02 on image cards, **tint** to Volt on CTA
- Reduced motion: all non-essential motion disabled via `prefers-reduced-motion`

### Condensed All-Caps Display Block
Nike's signature headline — massive condensed type in ALL CAPS, tight-tracked, with a Swoosh-scale weight that forces readers to look up.

## Iconography

Icons are linear, 1.75px stroke, sharp-cornered, and used sparingly — mostly for navigation, cart, and utility. Prefer Lucide at size 20–24px in white `#FFFFFF`, switching to Volt `#CDDC39` on hover.

- Treatment: **linear** outline, never filled or duotone
- Set: **lucide** (secondary fallback: feather)
- Stroke: **1.75px** uniform; avoid decorative variants

## Do's & Don'ts

### ✓ Do
- Anchor every page on a pure black or near-black ground and let white carry the voice
- Set all headlines in condensed ALL CAPS (Bebas Neue / Barlow Condensed 800–900) with tight tracking
- Treat the Swoosh as the only ornament; never decorate around it
- Use Volt `#CDDC39` as a single deliberate punch — focus rings, one hover state, one performance badge
- Commit to sharp edges: every button, input, card, and image at `border-radius: 0`

### ✗ Don't
- Don't use rounded corners — Nike is sharp-edged
- Don't reach for pastel or muted palettes
- Don't mix in serif fonts — the system is purely sans-condensed
- Don't soften the tone with playful or whimsical copy
- Don't add decorative ornament, patterns, glassmorphism, or blur
- Don't default to white page backgrounds; Nike marketing is dark-dominant

## Applications

Best fits: athletic and performance brands, sneaker drops and launch microsites, streetwear editorials, fitness and training apps, sports media hero pages, and any product story that wants a dark-dominant, photography-led, imperative voice. It is also a strong choice for portfolios or case studies that lead with a single bold image and want type to command rather than decorate.
